About AD Detailing

At AD Detailing, we are a South Shore–based business dedicated to delivering premium car detailing services. Our mission is simple: provide high-quality results that make your vehicle look and feel brand new.

We take pride in our attention to detail, using professional techniques and top-tier products to ensure every vehicle — car or RV — gets treated with care and precision.

Located in Châteauguay, we proudly serve the entire Rive-Sud MTL area. Whether it's a daily driver or a showroom piece, we treat every vehicle like our own.
